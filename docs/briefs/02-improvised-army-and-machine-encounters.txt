MECHANICA DESCENT

Improvised Army & Machine Encounters — Implementation Brief

Status: PROPOSED
Scope: Nine selected cards, companion AI, salvage systems, assembly mechanics, encounter branches, machine malfunctions, and Improvised Army combinations.

Implementation objective: Expand the player’s ability to assemble and maintain an improvised squad of scavenged machines, while making that squad vulnerable to enemy counterplay, resource shortages, corrupted programming, and unusual facility events.

This brief builds on the previously proposed Half-Awake Builder Drone, Salvage Leech, Wrong Hallway, and associated card interactions. It does not assume any unverified implementation progress.

Do not create an entirely separate combat system for the new cards. Integrate with existing turn sequencing, card costs, combat, damage, machine tags, encounter resolution, and player resources.

2. Shared machine architecture

These cards need common interaction rules so they behave like machinery occupying the same battlefield.

Machine states

Support the following states where applicable:

* Active: Able to act under ordinary rules.
* Inert: Present but unable to attack or move until activated.
* Repairing: Performing a maintenance operation.
* Carrying: Holding collected salvage.
* Protected: Assigned a defensive ally.
* Uncontrolled: Temporarily choosing actions without player direction.
* Immobilized: Cannot move or act for a specified duration.
* Shutdown: Temporarily inactive.
* Destroyed: Removed from active combat and potentially producing recoverable wreckage.

Store state changes on each machine instance, not globally on the card definition.

Action timing

Resolve a turn in the following order, adapting to the current engine’s equivalent event hooks:

1. Player actions and paid abilities.
2. Target validation and resource spending.
3. Movement, attacks, and normal damage.
4. Defensive interception and prevention effects at the appropriate pre-damage window.
5. Destruction reactions and wreckage generation.
6. Salvage collection opportunities.
7. Autonomous allied-machine actions.
8. Maintenance, assembly, and shutdown counters.
9. Enemy actions according to existing combat timing.
10. Duration expiration, cleanup, and encounter progression.

Avoid allowing the same autonomous unit to act twice because multiple events reference it.

Wreckage

Create a reusable wreckage record on eligible machine destruction.

Suggested properties:

* Source machine instance.
* Source faction.
* Original position or lane.
* Salvage eligibility.
* Collection status.
* Remaining recoverable value.
* Unique wreckage ID.

Every wreckage object must have a stable ID to prevent repeated collection.

If the game already represents destroyed units or dropped Scrap, use those records instead of duplicating them.

3. Card 1 — Scrap Shepherd

Type: Machine Ally
Rarity: Common
Cost: 2 Scrap
HP: 4
Attack: 0

Core ability — Wreckage Collector

Whenever an eligible machine is destroyed, the Scrap Shepherd attempts to retrieve its remains.

For every two distinct wrecks successfully collected, generate 2 Scrap.

The Shepherd stores collection progress independently from normal Scrap.

Behavior

When wreckage becomes available:

1. Check whether the Shepherd is active.
2. Identify eligible wreckage that has not been collected.
3. Prioritize the nearest accessible wreck.
4. Move toward it if movement is required.
5. Pick up the wreckage.
6. Increase its carried-wreck counter.
7. If the counter reaches two, convert the pair into 2 Scrap.
8. Reset the counter and resume searching.

If the game does not use positional movement, the Shepherd may retrieve one eligible wreck per autonomous action.

Carrying salvage

Show the Shepherd visibly accumulating metal plates, robot limbs, broken weapons, and other mechanical debris.

A Shepherd carrying one wreck has a meaningful vulnerability: it has not yet completed a profitable collection pair.

Destruction — Cargo Spill

When destroyed while carrying unfinished salvage:

* Drop its carried wreckage in a recoverable pile.
* Allow the player to collect it through normal salvage actions.
* Enemy scavengers may reach the pile first.
* Do not immediately grant the full two-wreck reward unless the requirement has been met.

The contents must retain their existing collected IDs so the player cannot repeatedly salvage the same wreck.

Player commands

If manual machine commands are supported, add:

Collect Nearest: Prioritize the nearest wreck.

Collect Selected: Target a specific eligible wreck.

Hold Position: Remain in place and collect only accessible salvage.

Retreat: Stop collecting and move toward safety.

Unload: Deliver carried salvage to a valid allied collection point.

Special behaviors

Under Fire: The Shepherd attempts to retain its cargo and move toward safer terrain, if a safe route exists.

Nearby Builder Drone: The two machines may perform a coordinated unloading action.

Enemy Salvager Present: Both factions may compete for the same wreckage.

Threat Beacon Present: The Shepherd may exploit enemies attacking the Beacon to retrieve exposed wrecks.

Builder Drone synergy — Assisted Unloading

A Builder Drone can spend one maintenance action assisting an adjacent or accessible Scrap Shepherd.

* If the Shepherd has two collected wrecks, complete unloading immediately.
* If it has one, secure that wreck against accidental cargo loss until the Shepherd moves or is destroyed.
* Do not generate Scrap without meeting the normal wreck threshold.

Safeguards

* Each wreck contributes to the Shepherd’s collection counter at most once.
* Destruction and rebuilding of an identical unit instance must not duplicate salvage.
* Enemy-generated temporary summons are eligible only when their normal destruction rules permit salvage.
* Wreckage used by another exclusive collector cannot simultaneously be collected by the Shepherd.
* Damage-based Salvage Leech extractions remain separate and do not require corpse ownership.

4. Card 2 — Ceiling Walker

Type: Machine Ally
Rarity: Uncommon
Cost: 2 Scrap
HP: 3
Attack: 2

Core ability — Elevated Traversal

The Ceiling Walker climbs above normal ground-level defenses.

While overhead:

* Ignore ordinary ground cover when determining its attack line.
* Avoid ground-only obstacles.
* Remain vulnerable to ranged attacks capable of targeting elevated units.
* Remain vulnerable to relevant explosions and environmental hazards.

Do not make it universally untargetable.

Behavior states

Climbing: Moves into an overhead position.

Hunting: Tracks an eligible enemy from above.

Attacking: Uses its normal 2-damage attack.

Scouting: Investigates a room feature instead of attacking.

Falling: Resolves its death effect.

Destruction — Falling Machine

If destroyed while overhead:

* Identify the closest eligible unit below.
* Deal 2 impact damage to that unit.
* Apply normal faction and environmental targeting rules.
* If no eligible unit exists, the Walker falls harmlessly.

Falling damage cannot trigger another fall from the same machine instance.

Player commands

Hunt: Seek the nearest valid enemy.

Perch: Remain at the current overhead position.

Scuttle: Move toward a selected valid location.

Scout: Investigate a designated feature.

Drop: Deliberately descend, forfeiting the overhead advantage until climbing again.

Special interaction — Wrong Hallway

During Something Behind You, the Walker can scout the source of the noise.

Possible results:

* Reveal the approaching enemy and its location.
* Identify damaged equipment on the enemy.
* Discover an alternate passage.
* Alert the enemy and lose the surprise advantage.

A failed scouting attempt should not automatically destroy the Walker.

Additional interactions

* Builder Drones can repair it when physically accessible.
* A destroyed Walker may produce wreckage for the Scrap Shepherd.
* Panic Jack can intercept eligible attacks against it while it is reachable.
* The Faulty Access Badge can improve its access to restricted maintenance passages, if the room supports them.

Visual behavior

The Walker should travel along overhead structures, suspend itself by articulated legs, fold around ceiling obstacles, and visibly lose grip when destroyed.

If the game has no ceiling geometry, implement an elevated-state indicator and appropriate targeting rules rather than requiring a new 3D traversal system.

5. Card 3 — Panic Jack

Type: Machine Ally
Rarity: Uncommon
Cost: 2 Scrap
HP: 5
Attack: 1

Core ability — Protective Panic

Panic Jack can assign one allied unit as its protected target.

Once per encounter, when an eligible enemy attack would damage that target, Panic Jack intercepts the attack.

Resolve the original attack against Panic Jack instead.

If Jack survives the intercepted damage, it immediately counterattacks the attacker for 2 damage, provided the attacker remains a valid target.

Target assignment

Support:

* Protect the player character, if the normal guard system allows it.
* Protect another machine ally.
* Protect a vulnerable deployed support unit.
* Clear the assignment.
* Reassign when command rules permit.

An interception requires a valid defensive position or an equivalent abstract guarding relationship.

Interception restrictions

* One interception per encounter.
* Does not prevent environmental damage that cannot be intercepted.
* Cannot intercept attacks targeting Jack itself.
* Does not intercept attacks that explicitly bypass guards.
* Cannot counterattack after being destroyed.
* Does not trigger another interception through its counterattack.

Secondary behavior — Panicked Retaliation

If its assigned protected ally is destroyed:

* Panic Jack becomes uncontrolled for one turn.
* Gain +2 damage to its normal attacks for the remainder of the encounter.
* Its next autonomous action prioritizes a valid enemy associated with the destruction.
* If that enemy is unavailable, select another valid hostile target.

The damage bonus is applied once and does not stack from subsequent losses.

Uncontrolled behavior

During the uncontrolled turn:

* Ignore ordinary movement and target commands.
* Prioritize attacking.
* Avoid deliberately attacking allies unless an existing malfunction system explicitly permits this.
* Return to normal command acceptance afterward.

Manual Override Hand interaction

Manual Override Hand can cancel the uncontrolled turn.

Doing so:

* Restores player control immediately.
* Preserves the +2 damage bonus.
* Causes the Hand’s normal 1 self-damage penalty.
* Consumes the machine’s ordinary action rather than granting a free additional attack.

Special reactions

Protected Builder Drone: Jack physically interposes itself between the drone and an attacker.

Protected Scrap Shepherd: Jack can save a valuable salvage carrier.

Protected Inert Warframe: Jack may guard the Warframe while its activation counter progresses.

Threat Beacon: Jack does not automatically protect a Beacon unless assigned.

Personality

Panic Jack should appear protective but excessively reactive.

Suggested animations:

* Sudden head turns toward nearby sounds.
* Oversized fist raised defensively.
* Frantic repositioning when its assigned unit is attacked.
* Brief loss of balance after intercepting a heavy blow.
* Aggressive, unstable movement after losing its protected ally.

6. Card 4 — Unfinished Warframe

Type: Rare Machine Ally
Cost: 3 Scrap
HP: 8
Attack: 3
Initial State: Inert

Core ability — Incomplete Startup

The Warframe enters play as an unfinished combat machine.

It cannot immediately attack or move.

It requires two activation progress points before becoming operational.

Activation rules

Two supported methods:

Passive Startup

* Gain one activation point at the end of each full owner turn after deployment.
* After two points, activate.

Builder Drone Commissioning

* A Builder Drone may spend a successful maintenance action working on the Warframe.
* Each such action grants one activation point.
* These points count toward the same two-point total.

The methods may combine. For example, one normal startup turn and one Builder Drone operation can complete activation.

Multiple drones can accelerate commissioning if their maintenance actions genuinely become available.

The player should be able to direct a maintenance-eligible drone toward commissioning, using an existing command or a contextual maintenance choice. This replaces that maintenance action; it is not free additional work.

Activation completion

When activation reaches two points:

1. Play the startup animation.
2. Change state from Inert to Active.
3. Enable normal Warframe commands.
4. Permit its first attack on its next eligible action.
5. Remove the startup progress indicator.

Do not allow an immediate bonus attack solely because the activation counter reached two.

Vulnerability while inert

The inactive Warframe:

* Can be damaged.
* Can be repaired.
* Can be protected by Panic Jack.
* Can be subjected to compatible enemy interference.
* Does not defend itself.

Corrupted Startup

If the Warframe is heavily damaged before activation, a corrupted startup may occur.

Proposed threshold: 3 HP or less at activation.

Proposed malfunction chance: 30%.

On failure, enter a temporary Hostile Startup state.

Hostile Startup behavior

* The Warframe changes allegiance temporarily.
* It chooses an eligible player-side target.
* It remains hostile until the player successfully resets its systems or the corruption expires.
* The condition must have a guaranteed finite resolution.

Suggested default: one hostile action, followed by a system stabilization check that returns control, unless a separate encounter explicitly establishes a longer malfunction.

Recovery options

Emergency Reset

* Spend 1 Energy to end hostile startup, if the player has an eligible control action.

Builder Drone Reset

* Spend a maintenance activation attempting to restore Warframe control.

Manual Override Hand

* Cancel the startup malfunction and immediately restore player control under the Hand’s normal rules.

Disable the Warframe

* Use standard control or damage mechanics to incapacitate it.

Do not require the Manual Override Hand as the only solution.

Additional options

Partial Activation

* With one progress point, the player may abort startup.
* Recover 1 Scrap.
* The Warframe remains inactive until normal activation resumes or the encounter ends.

Emergency Commissioning

* Spend 2 Energy to gain one activation point immediately.
* Once per deployed Warframe.
* Cannot exceed the two-point activation requirement.

These are optional player-facing mechanics that should be configurable.

Battlefield appearance

The Warframe should visibly become more complete during startup:

* Hydraulic pistons engage.
* Armor panels secure themselves.
* Internal lights activate.
* Weapon mounts align.
* Main combat systems initialize.
* An unstable startup produces a distinctly different warning sequence.

Do not require custom model-specific animations if the current models are temporary.

7. Card 6 — Faulty Access Badge

Type: Utility
Cost: 1 Energy

Core ability — Maintenance Authorization

Present a temporary facility credential to nearby compatible machines.

For two turns, eligible neutral maintenance systems recognize the player as authorized personnel.

Eligible targets

* Neutral Builder Drones.
* Maintenance terminals.
* Non-hostile repair machines.
* Industrial access systems.
* Compatible security checkpoints.

Do not automatically pacify actively hostile combat units.

Benefits

While the Badge is accepted:

* Neutral machines do not immediately treat ordinary proximity as trespassing.
* Certain maintenance interactions become available.
* The player may approach restricted equipment.
* The player may request limited repairs or information.
* Some access-related encounter options become easier.

Expiration — Security Verification

At the end of the second turn, affected systems perform a security verification.

Proposed baseline:

* 65%: Credential fails quietly; machines return to normal.
* 35%: Credential is flagged; increase Threat by 1.

Hostile player actions can increase detection probability.

Failed authorization reactions

* Maintenance drones stop cooperating.
* Security lights activate.
* Doors may lock according to existing room rules.
* Nearby machines may become suspicious.
* Some security-capable units investigate.

Do not automatically convert every neutral machine into an enemy.

Interactions

Builder Drone: Recognizes the player as a maintenance supervisor where its programming permits.

Scrap Shepherd: Can retrieve certain normally restricted wreckage.

Ceiling Walker: Gains access to an authorized maintenance route.

The Patient Machine: Unlocks an alternative diagnostic interaction.

Wrong Hallway: May allow the player to distinguish legitimate facility routes from anomalous passages.

Anti-exploit rules

* Reusing the Badge does not indefinitely extend authorization on the same machines.
* A failed credential remains flagged for the rest of that room.
* Attacking an authorized neutral machine cancels its trust.
* Credential checks must use seeded randomness.

8. Card 8 — Sealant Coffin

Type: Utility
Cost: 1 Energy

Core ability — Encapsulate Target

Cover an eligible machine in rapidly hardening industrial sealant.

The target becomes immobilized for two of its action opportunities.

At expiration, restore 3 HP if the target is still alive.

Friendly target

On an allied machine:

* Prevent movement.
* Prevent attacks and autonomous actions.
* Suspend ordinary maintenance actions.
* Preserve existing upgrades and attachments.
* Restore up to 3 HP when the sealant expires.

The target remains subject to applicable damage; the sealant is not invulnerability.

Enemy target

On an eligible enemy:

* Temporarily prevent movement and attacks.
* Apply normal resistance rules.
* Heal the enemy when the effect expires if it survives.

This makes the card an emergency disable with a potentially dangerous downside.

Proposed resistance

Target	Result
Standard mechanical enemy	Full two-action immobilization
Heavy mechanical enemy	One-action immobilization
Boss	One-action disruption only if susceptible
Explicitly immune target	No effect; reject invalid use before spending resources

Final resistances remain TUNE LATER.

Additional options

Release Early

* End the sealant effect prematurely.
* Restore only 1 HP.
* Useful when the player needs the machine to act immediately.

Keep Sealed

* Allow the normal two-action duration to complete.
* Restore the full 3 HP.

Break the Seal

* Affected enemies with a specific breakout capability may spend an action to remove the status.
* If successful, cancel the remaining duration and final healing.

Special interactions

Scrap Shepherd

* Can be sealed while carrying salvage.
* Retains cargo.
* Cannot collect additional wrecks.

Panic Jack

* Cannot intercept while immobilized.
* Preserves its assigned protection target.

Unfinished Warframe

* Passive startup pauses while sealed.
* Existing activation progress is retained.
* Repairs may still be applied only if existing status rules allow external repair.

Ceiling Walker

* May be sealed only while a valid target under the game’s positioning rules.
* Does not automatically fall merely because immobilized.

Builder Drone

* Maintenance cycle pauses during sealing.
* Resumes afterward without granting missed automatic actions.

Distinction from Maintenance Foam

Sealant Coffin is a two-action disable followed by substantial repair, while Maintenance Foam remains the previously proposed one-turn protection and minor repair tool.

Do not silently merge their effects.

9. Card 9 — Black-Box Recorder

Type: Nightmare Reaction
Cost: 1 Scrap

Trigger — Allied Machine Destroyed

When an allied machine is destroyed, offer Black-Box Recorder as a reaction if available.

Upon activation:

* Spend 1 Scrap.
* Draw 2 cards.
* Gain 1 Madness.
* Mark the effect as used for the encounter.

Restrictions

* Once per encounter across Black-Box Recorder activations.
* Do not trigger from discarded cards that were never deployed.
* Do not trigger from normal expiration of a temporary effect unless it was actually destroyed.
* Do not permit repeated triggering from the same destroyed machine.
* Apply ordinary hand-size and draw-pile rules.

Mechanical role

This card makes machine casualties tactically useful without preventing their consequences.

The player receives replacement options after losing an ally but pays in Madness.

Presentation

Display a small mechanical recording device replaying corrupted data from the destroyed machine.

Possible messages:

* “UNIT LOSS CONFIRMED.”
* “FINAL ACTION UNRECOVERABLE.”
* “REPLAYING LAST COMMAND.”
* “OPERATOR NOT FOUND.”
* “RECORDING CONTAINS UNKNOWN VOICE.”

These messages are atmospheric and do not establish that machines possess human memories or consciousness.

Special interactions

Panic Jack

* If destroyed during interception, Black-Box Recorder may trigger.

Unfinished Warframe

* Can record its destruction even before activation.

Scrap Shepherd

* Its wreckage remains available for separate salvage collection.

Dead Pilot’s Reflex

* Both reactions may become eligible from the same destruction.
* Resolve their order using a deterministic reaction queue.
* Neither reaction triggers itself.

10. Card 11 — Manual Override Hand

Type: Nightmare Equipment
Cost: 2 Scrap

Core ability — Direct Intervention

Once per encounter, cancel one eligible allied-machine malfunction and manually select that machine’s next ordinary action.

After the controlled action resolves, deal 1 damage to the machine.

Eligible malfunctions

* Possessed Builder wrong-target maintenance.
* Panic Jack temporary command loss.
* Unfinished Warframe corrupted startup.
* Other explicitly tagged allied-machine malfunctions.

The Hand must not function as unrestricted mind control over enemy units.

Activation sequence

1. Detect a malfunction about to resolve or currently preventing an eligible command.
2. Show the Manual Override opportunity.
3. Player selects Override.
4. Cancel the malfunction’s immediate negative behavior.
5. Choose one legal normal action for the affected machine.
6. Resolve that action.
7. Apply 1 damage to the machine.
8. Mark the Hand as used for the encounter.

If the machine is destroyed by the self-damage, process destruction and wreckage normally.

Targeted interactions

Possessed Builder

* Prevent an unwanted enemy repair.
* Choose an allied repair target instead.

Panic Jack

* Cancel its uncontrolled turn.
* Direct its next normal action.

Unfinished Warframe

* Restore control after a corrupted startup.
* Allow ordinary Warframe action timing to resume.

Scrap Shepherd

* Not normally eligible because autonomous scavenging is not a malfunction.

Ceiling Walker

* Not normally eligible simply because the player dislikes its chosen attack.

Longer-term machine reactions

PROPOSED optional atmospheric feature:

Track the number of Manual Override uses during a run.

At higher counts, machines may:

* Hesitate before receiving commands.
* Produce unusual diagnostics.
* Display unexplained recognition messages.
* Briefly attempt to return to previous autonomous priorities.

Do not introduce an unavoidable combat penalty without a separate balance decision.

These are presentation and event hooks, not confirmed lore.

11. Card 14 — The Patient Machine

Type: Rare Encounter
Theme: Abandoned repair ward / disabled combat machine

Encounter setup

The player discovers a large damaged machine connected to an industrial maintenance cradle.

Its combat systems are offline.

Its diagnostic screen repeatedly requests repair.

The machine may display fragments of corrupted operational records, damaged mission instructions, or incomplete warnings.

It is not initially hostile.

Player options

Implement four primary approaches.

A. Repair the Patient

Spend 2 Scrap, or complete two eligible Builder Drone maintenance actions.

On success:

* Restore the machine to basic functionality.
* Receive a temporary allied machine for the current encounter or room sequence.
* Proposed stats: 6 HP / 2 damage.
* Unlock a small dialogue or diagnostic response.

Proposed danger: 20% chance its damaged combat systems reactivate aggressively during repair.

If hostile activation occurs, the player may disable or stabilize it rather than automatically lose the reward.

B. Strip It for Parts

Carefully dismantle the damaged machine.

Reward:

* Gain 4 Scrap.
* Chance to recover one uncommon compatible mechanical component or card reward.

Proposed danger: 30% chance the defensive system awakens during dismantling.

The player must finish the hostile encounter or abandon the salvage operation.

Do not guarantee both a full salvage payout and a rare item on every visit.

C. Inspect Its Memory

Spend 1 Energy to recover diagnostic information.

Possible results:

* Reveal a hidden route clue.
* Learn information about a nearby machine encounter.
* Identify a maintenance cache.
* Discover a warning about an anomaly.
* Unlock an alternate event resolution.

Proposed danger: 25% chance accessing damaged records reactivates a defensive routine.

Do not use machine records to reveal unapproved story canon.

D. Leave It Alone

Decline the encounter.

* No major reward.
* No combat.
* Continue along the current route.

The player may still receive a short atmospheric message as they leave.

Special approaches

Faulty Access Badge

* Attempt authorized diagnostic access.
* Reduce the likelihood of an aggressive security response.
* Does not guarantee success.

Builder Drone

* Perform repair gradually.
* May reveal a hidden fault before full activation.
* Can become a target if the Patient awakens.

Scrap Shepherd

* Assist in collecting salvage after legitimate dismantling.
* Do not permit repeated collection of the Patient’s same components.

Ceiling Walker

* Inspect overhead diagnostic machinery or inaccessible cables.
* Reveal one hazard before choosing the primary approach.

Manual Override Hand

* If the Patient is repaired and temporarily allied, the Hand may correct an eligible malfunction.
* Cannot simply seize control of the neutral Patient before a repair or encounter rule makes it an ally.

Black-Box Recorder

* If the temporarily allied Patient is later destroyed, its destruction may satisfy the Recorder’s normal trigger.

Patient Machine identity

Keep its nature deliberately unresolved.

It may be:

* A damaged industrial machine.
* An abandoned combat frame.
* A machine preserving old operator instructions.
* A malfunctioning repair construct.
* Something more unusual.

Do not decide that it is conscious, haunted, or connected to a particular established character without further canon approval.

12. Improvised Army synergy package

Concept

The player constructs a small, increasingly capable fighting force from damaged machinery and battlefield salvage.

Rather than simply playing expensive combat units, the player assembles a support chain:

Builder Drone → Unfinished Warframe → Scrap Shepherd

The Builder Drone accelerates construction and keeps machines repaired.

The Warframe becomes the squad’s primary combat unit.

The Scrap Shepherd gathers wreckage to finance replacements and repairs.

Panic Jack, Ceiling Walker, and the selected equipment cards can extend this approach.

12.1 Squad roles

Unit	Role
Builder Drone	Maintenance and commissioning
Scrap Shepherd	Wreck collection
Unfinished Warframe	Heavy combat
Panic Jack	Protecting vulnerable machines
Ceiling Walker	Scouting and elevated attacks

No new faction system is required.

These are ordinary allied machines using existing allegiance rules.

12.2 Coordinated commands

When two or more eligible allied machines are deployed, provide contextual cooperation commands where the existing command interface supports them.

Commission Warframe

Order a Builder Drone to spend its maintenance action advancing a Warframe’s activation.

Protect Assembly

Assign Panic Jack to guard an inert Warframe or the Builder Drone working on it.

Collect Battlefield Wreckage

Order the Scrap Shepherd to prioritize recoverable debris after a machine is destroyed.

Scout Ahead

Use Ceiling Walker to investigate hazards or enemies before exposing the squad.

Emergency Recovery

Apply Sealant Coffin to a badly damaged key machine when temporarily losing its actions is worth the repair.

These commands are contextual uses of existing card abilities, not an additional independent action economy.

12.3 Improvised Army special interaction — Assembly Line

PROPOSED optional synergy:

When a Builder Drone and an Unfinished Warframe are both present, the Warframe gains an Assembly Line indicator.

The indicator tracks completed maintenance work.

With a Scrap Shepherd also present:

* The Shepherd may deliver collected wreckage to the Warframe.
* Two delivered wrecks can substitute for one Builder Drone commissioning action.
* Doing so consumes those wrecks without generating the Shepherd’s usual 2 Scrap payout.
* This contribution can occur only once per Warframe deployment.

This provides an important decision:

Use salvage as money, or sacrifice it to activate combat power sooner?

Do not grant both benefits.

12.4 Improvised Army special encounter — The Assembly Yard

Add a rare optional event to suitable industrial facility rooms.

Setup

The player encounters an abandoned machine assembly station containing:

* Wreckage piles.
* Functional industrial arms.
* Damaged frames.
* An unstable power connection.
* Potentially hostile maintenance security.

The event becomes richer when the player has a Builder Drone, Scrap Shepherd, or Unfinished Warframe.

Option A — Scavenge the Yard

* Gather a moderate Scrap reward.
* Risk activating salvage-defense machinery.
* Shepherd improves collection efficiency.

Option B — Commission a Warframe

* Use the assembly station to add one activation progress point to an existing inert Warframe.
* May require 1 Energy.
* Risk triggering a security check.

Option C — Restore Station Power

* Spend 2 Scrap.
* Restore an industrial machine or support station.
* Gain a temporary maintenance benefit.
* Risk activating an unwanted machine.

Option D — Sabotage the Yard

* Destroy the power system.
* Prevent hostile machinery from activating.
* Lose access to assembly-related rewards.

Option E — Use False Authorization

If Faulty Access Badge is available:

* Attempt to pass the station security check.
* Gain access to safer interaction choices.
* Risk increasing Threat on credential failure.

Option F — Leave

Decline the encounter and preserve resources.

12.5 Improvised Army combat complications

Introduce some enemy responses to machinery-heavy player builds.

Enemy Recovery Crew

* Attempts to retrieve useful wreckage before the Shepherd reaches it.

Machine Saboteur

* Targets an unfinished Warframe’s startup mechanism.

Disruption Unit

* Attempts to disable Builder Drone maintenance for one turn.

Heavy Assault Enemy

* Prioritizes the active Warframe when it becomes the most significant threat.

Security Scanner

* Detects suspicious machine-authority credentials or unauthorized construction activity.

Use these behaviors only for compatible enemies and encounters.

Do not universally punish players merely for using several machine cards.

13. Worked combat example

Initial state

The player possesses:

* Half-Awake Builder Drone.
* Scrap Shepherd.
* Unfinished Warframe.
* Panic Jack.
* Sealant Coffin.
* Black-Box Recorder.

The room contains several hostile security machines.

Turn 1

Deploy Unfinished Warframe.

* Warframe begins inert at 0/2 activation progress.
* Panic Jack is assigned to guard it.
* Builder Drone remains available for maintenance.
* Scrap Shepherd searches for available wreckage.

At the appropriate startup step, Warframe gains its first passive progress point.

Turn 2

An enemy attacks the Warframe.

* Panic Jack intercepts the attack if its defensive conditions are satisfied.
* Jack survives and counterattacks for 2 damage.
* A Builder Drone may spend an available maintenance action on commissioning.
* If that completes 2/2 progress, the Warframe activates.

The newly active Warframe waits until its next eligible normal action to attack.

Turn 3

The Warframe attacks an enemy for 3 damage.

An enemy is destroyed.

* Generate eligible wreckage.
* Scrap Shepherd begins collection.
* If two qualifying wrecks have been collected, gain 2 Scrap.

The player now has greater offensive capability, but the Warframe is exposed.

Turn 4

The Warframe becomes heavily damaged.

The player chooses Sealant Coffin.

* Warframe cannot act while sealed.
* Panic Jack may continue guarding it if eligible.
* Builder Drone performs other maintenance.
* Sealant Coffin eventually restores 3 HP.

The player sacrifices short-term offense for future survivability.

Turn 5

A support machine is destroyed.

Black-Box Recorder becomes available.

The player activates it:

* Spend 1 Scrap.
* Draw 2 cards.
* Gain 1 Madness.

The army loses a valuable machine, but the player receives new options for continuing the encounter.

This is the desired gameplay cycle: build, protect, salvage, suffer losses, and improvise.

⸻

14. Deterministic implementation and state handling

Each new card should expose its runtime state clearly.

Suggested state fields:

System	State to track
Scrap Shepherd	Collected wreck IDs, carried count, current collection target
Ceiling Walker	Ground/elevated state, current target, scouting state
Panic Jack	Protected target, interception used, uncontrolled turns, retaliation bonus
Unfinished Warframe	Activation progress, startup state, corrupted startup result
Faulty Access Badge	Affected machines, remaining turns, security-check result
Sealant Coffin	Target, remaining action opportunities, early release
Black-Box Recorder	Encounter-use flag, destruction event reference
Manual Override Hand	Encounter-use flag, pending overridden action
Patient Machine	Encounter branch, interaction progress, generated rewards, defensive response

All state changes should be event-driven and saved when the current game supports mid-encounter saving.

Combat safeguards

* Prevent repeated wreck collection.
* Prevent unlimited Warframe activation from one Builder Drone operation.
* Prevent Panic Jack from intercepting more than one attack per encounter.
* Prevent Ceiling Walker from falling multiple times.
* Prevent repeated Black-Box draws from the same destruction.
* Prevent Manual Override from granting an extra machine action.
* Prevent Sealant Coffin from stacking immobilization indefinitely.
* Prevent Patient Machine reward duplication.
* Prevent repeated Faulty Access Badge use from creating permanent authorization.

⸻

15. Testing requirements

Machine behavior tests

* Scrap Shepherd collects two eligible wrecks and grants exactly 2 Scrap.
* A destroyed Shepherd spills unfinished cargo correctly.
* Ceiling Walker ignores ordinary ground cover but remains vulnerable to valid attacks.
* A destroyed overhead Walker causes exactly one falling-damage event.
* Panic Jack intercepts once, takes the intended damage, and counterattacks only if alive.
* Panic Jack becomes temporarily uncontrolled after its protected ally is destroyed.
* Unfinished Warframe activates after two normal startup increments.
* Builder Drone commissioning advances the same startup counter.
* Damaged Warframe startup can produce a deterministic corruption result.

Equipment and reaction tests

* Faulty Access Badge affects neutral eligible machinery, not arbitrary hostiles.
* Authorization expires after two turns and performs exactly one security check.
* Sealant Coffin blocks two eligible action opportunities and restores HP at normal expiration.
* Enemy healing after Sealant Coffin expiration works correctly.
* Black-Box Recorder activates once per encounter.
* Manual Override Hand cancels an eligible malfunction and applies 1 self-damage after the chosen action.
* Manual Override Hand does not create an extra action or override an ordinary AI decision without a malfunction.

Encounter tests

* Patient Machine resolves all four main choices.
* Builder Drone repairs can complete the Patient Machine restoration requirement.
* Salvage rewards cannot be collected repeatedly.
* Faulty Access Badge unlocks the intended authorization option.
* Patient Machine defensive activation produces a resolvable encounter.
* Assembly Yard supports its different branches.
* Assembly Line consumes wreckage instead of granting duplicate Scrap.
* Existing room completion remains functional after special encounters.

Integration tests

Run the machine cards together against:

* Ordinary ranged enemies.
* Melee attackers.
* Heavy mechanical opponents.
* Repair-capable enemies.
* Enemies that collect salvage.
* Environmental hazards.
* Wrong Hallway events.
* Extended encounters with insufficient Scrap.

Observe whether the Improvised Army is viable without becoming self-sustaining indefinitely.

16. Suggested implementation order

Phase 1 — Basic allied machines

Implement Scrap Shepherd, Ceiling Walker, Panic Jack, and Unfinished Warframe with their core autonomous actions and status transitions.

Phase 2 — Support utilities

Implement Faulty Access Badge, Sealant Coffin, Black-Box Recorder, and Manual Override Hand.

Phase 3 — Encounter content

Implement all Patient Machine branches and their interactions with the new cards.

Phase 4 — Improvised Army integration

Add coordinated commissioning, salvage delivery, contextual commands, and the Assembly Yard event.

Phase 5 — Enemy response and polish

Implement compatible enemy counterplay, contextual dialogue, animations, deterministic test cases, and balance adjustments.

17. Acceptance criteria

The expansion is ready for an initial playable test when:

1. All nine selected cards work using existing game systems.
2. Each machine has a recognizable tactical function.
3. Scrap Shepherd can retrieve and unload wreckage without duplication.
4. Ceiling Walker can attack from elevated positions and perform scouting.
5. Panic Jack correctly protects allies and reacts to losing them.
6. Unfinished Warframe supports normal startup, Builder Drone commissioning, and corrupted startup.
7. Faulty Access Badge temporarily changes eligible neutral-machine behavior.
8. Sealant Coffin supports allied healing and enemy immobilization with appropriate costs.
9. Black-Box Recorder and Manual Override Hand resolve correctly through existing event hooks.
10. The Patient Machine has its complete interactive encounter and risk/reward branches.
11. Improvised Army supports cooperative salvage, commissioning, protection, and battlefield adaptation.
12. All rewards, temporary allies, and encounter outcomes have bounded, reproducible behavior.

OPEN / TUNE LATER

Keep these decisions explicitly unresolved:

* Final Scrap costs and encounter frequency.
* Exact ceiling and elevation mechanics.
* Warframe corruption threshold and probability.
* Badge authorization failure rates.
* Patient Machine reward rarity.
* Whether longer machine-memory consequences should have mechanical effects.
* How much command control autonomous allies should receive.
* Final Scrap generation rate for sustained machine-heavy decks.
* Whether Assembly Line requires specific cards or becomes a more general support mechanic.
* Whether the Patient Machine’s records have any eventual narrative significance.

Design target: The player should feel increasingly capable of assembling a little army from whatever survives the descent. The machines should be useful individually, stronger when coordinated, and vulnerable in ways that create interesting decisions rather than arbitrary punishment.

A successful Improvised Army run should tell a small mechanical story: rescuing an unfinished Warframe, protecting it while it awakens, gathering broken parts to keep the squad running, and deciding how much control to exert when its machinery begins behaving strangely.
